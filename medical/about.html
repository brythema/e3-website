What the experience can connect

More connected care for every part of the service.

The exact setup is agreed with each service. These are the key outcomes the E³ medical experience is designed to support.

01

For patients

An AI nurse that reviews their medical history before it recommends anything, drug orders from the partner pharmacy, a chatroom to the doctor and reminders that keep doses on time. Care between visits.

02

For doctors & nurses

Fewer repeated questions and scattered calls. Routine guidance is held by the AI layer while the chatroom keeps the conversations that need a professional in one place.

03

For pharmacies

Drug orders arrive digitally instead of at the counter alone, with a direct line to the patients they serve. A wider reach without wider queues.

04

For clinics & hospitals

Continuity between appointments: history-aware support covers the hours staff cannot, and follow-ups stay connected instead of falling through.

05

For treatment plans

Plans are more likely to be finished when refills are one order away and doses are reminded on schedule. Better adherence, fewer avoidable gaps.

06

For the service system

Let E³ manage the agreed technology setup, maintenance and improvements, rather than leaving the service to coordinate every technical detail itself. Technology support.

Between visits

Backup knowledge for the hours nobody else is available.

The AI nurse is the first line of support when a professional is not in the room. It is designed to ground every suggestion in the patient’s own record before anything else.

HISTORY FIRSTIt reviews the patient’s medical history before it recommends anything, so guidance builds on what is already known.
PRACTICAL GUIDANCEClear next steps for common needs—what to do, what to watch and when it is time for a professional.
HANDOVERWhen the case needs a human, the patient moves into the chatroom with the doctor—no restart, no lost context.

The AI nurse is an assistance layer, not a medical professional. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional care, and urgent situations belong with the professionals behind the service.

Questions services ask

Before we begin.

A few practical answers to help you decide whether to start a conversation.

Setup

Do we need to build our own health application?

+

No. E³’s model is to provide the agreed digital infrastructure and support so the service does not have to coordinate every part of development and maintenance independently. The exact platform setup and responsibilities will be agreed with you.

E³ note
AI nurse

Does the AI nurse replace doctors and nurses?

+

No. It supports the hours when staff are not available, reviews the patient’s history before suggesting anything and hands the patient back to the professionals through the chatroom. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional care.

E³ note
Records

What happens to a patient’s medical history?

+

The AI nurse reviews the history recorded within the service so its suggestions fit the patient in front of it. Data handling, consent and access are agreed with the partner service, and guidance is based on the patient’s own record.

E³ note
Orders

How do online drug orders work?

+

Patients place their request in the platform and it moves to the partner pharmacy, which confirms and fulfils it. Fulfilment depends on the pharmacy’s stock and workflow.

E³ note
Chatroom

Can patients really reach their doctor?

+

The chatroom is designed to keep the doctor–patient conversation in one place instead of scattered calls and visits. Response times depend on each professional’s availability—which is exactly the gap the AI nurse covers in between.

E³ note

Pricing depends on which core experience and optional add-ons your service chooses, and is discussed with you before any commitment. Nothing is included unless it is confirmed in the partnership agreement.
