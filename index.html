The E³ way

Start with the problem. Then earn the technology.

We are not interested in putting technology on an organisation just because technology is available. We look at what people are trying to do, where the friction lives and what a better experience should feel like.

That means we may recommend a simple first step instead of a giant build. The work can grow in practical phases as the organisation grows with it.
