The platform

Abuba Events—discovery and ticketing in one hub.

The app is where the night starts on the screen. It is designed to keep the path from curiosity to confirmation obvious at every step.

DISCOVERThe catalog shows what is on, when it starts and what to expect, so the decision happens without asking around first.
DECIDEEach event has its own page—details, mood and ticket plans together, so the practical and the atmosphere meet.
BOOKThe ticket is guaranteed by the platform and the numbers hold, so a booked place is a real one.

Abuba Events is a discovery and booking layer, not a promoter. The organiser runs the night—the platform makes it easier to find, easier to book and easier to trust.

Questions venues ask

Before we begin.

A few practical answers to help you decide whether to start a conversation.

Setup

Do we need to build our own event application?

+

No. Abuba Events is the shared discovery and ticketing layer—the venue or organiser lists the event and sells tickets through it, and E³ manages the agreed setup. The exact platform configuration and responsibilities are agreed with you.

E³ note
Organisers

Can organisers list events and sell tickets?

+

Yes. Organisers list events through the organiser flow, add ticket plans that power the sales buttons, and put their brand on a profile the platform manages. E³ keeps the event’s details and media current.

E³ note
Ticketing

What happens when a night sells out?

+

The platform guarantees the tickets and holds the numbers, so the same place is not sold twice. A sold-out night is fair—and the people who booked actually get in.

E³ note
Payment

How does the buyer pay?

+

Payment in the current setup is by bank transfer, confirmed on the platform’s dashboard. E³ keeps the flow practical for the market it serves, and each organiser’s confirmation process is agreed during setup.

E³ note
Trust

How does the crowd know an event is real?

+

Events are listed by organisers on a profile the platform manages, and the tickets are guaranteed by the platform. The exact trust setup is agreed per organiser—and it gets stronger as the scene grows.

E³ note
Pricing

How much does the partnership cost?

+

Pricing depends on which core experience and optional add-ons the venue or organiser chooses, and is discussed with you before any commitment. Nothing is included unless it is confirmed in the partnership agreement.
