Questions school leaders ask

Before we begin.

A few practical answers to help you decide whether to start a conversation.

Setup

Do we need to build our own school application?

+

No. E³’s model is to provide the agreed digital infrastructure and support so the school does not have to coordinate every part of development and maintenance independently. The exact platform setup and responsibilities will be agreed with your school.

E³ note
Results

Can parents really see results before the end of term?

+

The platform is designed to give authorised parents access to results as the school records and updates them. It does not generate assessment data that the school has not entered, and availability depends on the school’s setup and workflow.

E³ note
Workflow

Does the system calculate results automatically?

+

It is designed to calculate results from entered scores using the school’s configured grading rules. The school remains responsible for entering accurate scores and confirming its academic policies.

E³ note
Pricing

How much does a school partnership cost?

+

The subscription starts at ₦100,000. The final cost depends on which core services and optional add-ons your school chooses. We discuss the scope and pricing with you before any commitment.

E³ note
Add-ons

Can E³ manage our social media and create graphics?

+

Yes, these can be discussed as optional add-on services. Website management, social media publishing, graphic design, audio, video, animation and other digital support are scoped to your school’s needs and quoted accordingly.

E³ note
Opportunities

Are scholarships and paid internships guaranteed?

+

No. E³ aims to connect eligible students to relevant opportunities as programme arrangements become available. Selection, eligibility, partner availability and programme terms apply, and individual outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
