The real school challenge

Parents shouldn’t have to wait until the end of term to know how their child is doing.

A school is more than a website or an app. It is a living relationship between learners, parents, teachers and school leadership—so the digital layer should help that relationship, not strain it.

01

Academic concerns can stay hidden too long.

When families only see a complete picture at the end of term, they can miss the chance to ask questions and support a child earlier. E³ is designed to give parents access to recorded assessment results as the school enters and updates them.

02

Teachers have enough to do without unnecessary arithmetic.

Manually calculating totals, averages and grades adds repetitive work to a teacher’s day. With the school’s grading rules configured, the platform can calculate results from the scores entered—helping reduce avoidable manual calculation.

03

Communication shouldn’t start only when something goes wrong.

Parents and teachers need a clearer way to discuss progress, ask questions and stay connected to a child’s school experience. A connected digital workflow helps make those conversations easier to initiate and follow up.

04

Students need opportunities they may never hear about.

Digital skills, learning communities, scholarships and career pathways can feel out of reach when students do not know where to begin. E³ aims to help partner schools connect promising learners with relevant resources and opportunities.

The E³ school portfolio

Not just a school website. A more useful digital layer.

Start with the tools your school needs today. Add services as your goals grow. E³ works with your school to create a clearer digital experience for families, staff and students.

01

Your school’s digital front door

Present your school, programmes, facilities, admissions, announcements and identity in a clear, mobile-friendly experience that helps families understand what you offer and what to do next.

02

Results parents can follow

Give authorised parents a route to view the academic results the school has recorded, without making the end-of-term report their only window into performance.

03

Less repeated work for staff

Support a simpler results workflow with system-calculated totals and grades based on configured rules, alongside clearer digital communication and school information.

04

Learning beyond the classroom

Give eligible students access to the E³ Learning Hub—courses in artificial intelligence and digital marketing with guided practical projects, alongside learning routes into programming, automation and machine learning.
