Digital presence

Why your homepage should not try to explain everything

+

The visitor already arrived with a reason. Give that reason a clear path instead of making the homepage carry every detail about the organisation.

E³ note
Technology

Build around the problem—not the tool

+

The right technology is often the consequence of a clear problem definition. Start with the people, friction and desired outcome.

E³ note
SMEs

What “international standard” should mean online

+

It does not mean pretending to be somewhere else. It means being clear, usable, credible, responsive and intentional about the people you want to serve.

E³ note
Schools

A school website is more than a noticeboard

+

Parents are not only reading. They are deciding whether they trust the school, how easily they can reach it and what their next action should be.

E³ note
AI

Where AI actually belongs in a small organisation

+

Good AI does not need to be everywhere. It should quietly improve the parts of work that are repetitive, information-heavy or slow.

E³ note
Growth

Start small. Design for the next version.

+

A practical first release can be more valuable than a giant system that takes too long to become useful. The important thing is to leave room for the organisation to grow.
