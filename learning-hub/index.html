Mentorship & scholarships

A route for the bright minds.

E³ aims to identify standout students and connect them with people who practise these skills—guidance on their work and their direction, one-to-one or in small groups, as arrangements allow.

MENTORSHIPStandout students can be matched with E³ mentors for guidance on their work and next steps.

IN PRACTICEThese are opportunity routes, not guarantees of mentorship, scholarships or placement. Eligibility, availability and terms apply.

Who can access

Two doors. One standard.

Subscribing schools extend Learning Hub access to their students as part of the partnership. Individuals can also subscribe to E³ directly and learn on the same standard. E³ manages who is inside the hub—approvals, content, standards and updates.

SCHOOLSSubscribing schools extend hub access to their students at the agreed seat count.
INDIVIDUALSLearners can subscribe directly to E³ and learn on the same standard.
E³-MANAGEDAccess, content, standards and progress are administered by E³—not by the school.

Same principle as the rest of E³→the hospital connects to its pharmacy application; the academy connects to its school application; the Learning Hub connects students to their next step.
